Global conductor intermediate order on the six ruled surfaces
===========================================================

Let X be the smooth pinned sextic CY4 and D its integral degree32 source-bound
divisor. Set b=Y1 and S=Y0^6+Y2^6. Rank/fresh constructed the exact expression
  D=q*S^2+b*r*S+b^2*s,
with deg(q,r,s)=(20,25,30). The degree25 rational function
  theta=q*S/b
is integral: theta^2+r*theta+q*s=0. Moreover
  b*theta=q*S,  S*theta=-r*S-b*s.
The finite intermediate order O_D[theta] is generated by1,theta. Its quotient
by O_D is annihilated by (b,S), hence receives O_T(-25), T=(b,S)⊂X.
At each generic component of T the split-node calculation shows theta adds
one independent normalization direction. Since T is reduced CM with no
embedded primes, the kernel inside O_T(-25) vanishes if it vanishes at every
component's generic point. Thus
  0 -> O_D -> O_D[theta] -> O_T(-25) -> 0
is exact. This is an intermediate repair, not a claim of complete normalization.

T is a CI(1,6) in X, equivalently CI(6,6) in P4, so it is ACM and
  omega_T=O_T(7).
Its six components are the ruled sextic cones Y0=aY2,Y1=0,a^6=-1. On one
component omega=O(2); the difference of5 is the other five components meeting
along Gamma={Y0=Y1=Y2=0}. This explains why a generic single-surface O(-30)
conductor frame becomes the global union frame O_T(-25).

The conductor ideal of this intermediate order is (b,S). Finite duality gives
  Hom_D(O_D[theta],omega_D)=(b,S)*omega_D,
so the dual connecting map is the literal restriction
  H0 O_D(32) -> H0 O_T(32)
(up to the fixed nonzero generator normalization). This map is surjective by
the CI ideal resolution. Since H1 O_T(-25)=0 and H1 O_D=H2 O_D=0, the
intermediate order has H1=H2=0.

This does not handle the separate codimension-one Fermat center X0=X1=0,
where D has multiplicity17, nor any subsequent resolution defects. Additional
normalization quotients supported only in dimension<=1 cannot by themselves
create H2 from this intermediate order, but another surface quotient can.
