Global adjoint map for the source-bound physical point cover
==========================================================

Base and physical finite-flat order
----------------------------------
B=P2_(Y0,Y1,Y2) x P1_(X0,X1), bidegrees ordered (Y,X).
On Y2=X0=1, a=Y0/Y2,b=Y1/Y2,v=X1/X0.  Let
  tau=t_old/h^2=n/d
be the reduced fraction in effective_physical.pickle.  The exact degrees are
  M=deg(d)=(15,17), deg(n)=M+T=(13,19), T=(-2,2), F=(0,1).
The physical fibre coordinate w=X2/h has bundle F, and tau has bundle T.
Set R=d*w, Z=d*w^2.  Then R,Z have bundles M+F,M+2F.  The global equations are
  R^2=d*Z,
  Z^3+c*(X0^3*d+Bphys*n)*R*Z
       +P*d^3+Q*d^2*n+Rphys*d*n^2+S*n^3=0.
Here the physical coefficients have bidegrees
  Bphys=(2,1), P=(0,6), Q=(2,4), Rphys=(4,2), S=(6,0).
All displayed terms are regular global sections of the appropriate bundles.
The leading monomials R^2,Z^3 are coprime and monic, so the order is finite flat
of degree6, free in basis 1,Z,Z^2,R,RZ,RZ^2.  This assertion does not require
normality.  The dualizing bundle is
  omega_Y=pi^*(K_B+3M+5F)=pi^*O_B(42,54).
Its canonical residue frame has mu3 character chi.  The function characters
of the free basis are (0,2,1,1,0,2).
Consequently the raw order has H1(O)=H2(O)=0 by the six negative monomial
bundle summands; canonical chi sections are
  H0 O_B(42,54) + RZ*H0 O_B(12,17), dimension 53668.
NORMALITY and resolution corrections are separate requirements.

Local cone, correct coherent target, and duality
-----------------------------------------------
At a transverse common zero of d,n away the proper-curve discriminants,
the transverse singularity is the normal canonical genus4 cone.  Its ordinary
blowup has discrepancy -2.  Therefore its local adjoint ideal is I^2,
I=(Z,R,d,n), and its canonical defect is omega_Y/I^2 omega_Y.
This is a FIRST-JET MODULE, not five independent line bundles on the reduced
common-zero curve.  Its relevant chi component consists of the constant,
d-linear and n-linear jets of the canonical residue frame.  Equivalently,
  Q_chi=O_B(42,54) tensor O_B/(d,n)^2
on this cone model.  The R1(O) dual module has nontrivial I-action from its
H1(O_C) layer to its H1(O_C(1)) layer and is killed by I^2.

For projective normal Gorenstein Y and a resolution f:X->Y, GR vanishing gives
H1(omega_X)=H1(f_*omega_X).  If H1(omega_Y)=0, the exact sequence of adjoints
identifies H1(omega_X) with the cokernel of
  H0(omega_Y) -> H0(omega_Y/f_*omega_X).
In character chi, this is dual to H2(O_X)_(chi^2), hence has the dimension of
H20(X)_chi needed by the actual target.  Thus it is the canonical restriction
map, not the local rank5 defect count, that decides global serving forms.

Explicit cone-only interpolation
--------------------------------
For the full complete-intersection ideal J=(d,n), the square has the twisted
Hilbert-Burch resolution
  0 -> O(-1,1)+O(1,-1)
    -> O(12,20)+O(14,18)+O(16,16)
    -> J^2*O(42,54) -> 0.
Every higher cohomology group of the displayed five bundles vanishes.
Thus H1(J^2*O(42,54))=0, and all first jets interpolate.  The jet target has
dimension45238; the kernel of the full canonical-chi map has dimension8430
(including the RZ summand, which maps to zero).  This closes the smooth-cone
contribution alone.  It does NOT prove H20=0 for a normalization/resolution
with additional conductor, discriminant, or corner adjoint conditions.
Those conditions may restrict the global canonical-section source before
restriction to the cone jets.  The normalization of selected common-zero
components need not have the arithmetic genus of the full CI.
