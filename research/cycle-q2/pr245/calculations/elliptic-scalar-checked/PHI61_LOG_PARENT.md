# Logarithmic parent of the polar Newton differential

Set Θ=(1/(3b³)) dlog T. At fixed T,
D_J b=b⁹T/P_b, hence ∇JΘ=−b⁵ dT/P_b=φ₆₁ literally.
More generally the fixed-T representative for any i≠9 is
−b^(i−9)T^(j−2)dT/(i−9). Its I derivative is −J_I φᵢⱼ. In particular,
I∇I Θ=−2Θ−(I/6)φ₅₁−2φ₉₀ follows directly from the quartic and its first derivatives.

At a bottom edge point b_i³=−1 with ord T=1, the residue of Θ is −1/3. At the two infinities with b of order −2 the form is regular. On the b=0 edge b=s⁴, T=c s⁹ U(s), c⁴=−I⁻⁶, U(0)=1. Dividing the physical quartic by s³⁶ shows that log U has coefficient 1/4 at s¹²: the preceding perturbations start at orders 5 and 9, and no sum of positive orders 5 and 9 equals 12. Consequently the residue of Θ there is +1. All residues are constant in I,J. The divisor of residues is a nontrivial class modulo logarithms of torus units. Such units are monomials modulo moduli constants (TORIC_TAME.md). Bottom and (−2,−3) valuations of b^aT^c would force c=−1/3, a=1/2, which at (−2,−9) gives 2 whereas Θ has residue zero. Therefore this section genuinely adds a logarithmic residue direction to the proper genus-ten system.

The quasi-Euler transport in the parent thread gives, in relative cohomology,
ξ=−11Iφ₅₂/6−4φ₉₁+3Jφ₆₂−6φ₆₁.
Using κ=Tdb/b⁵, ∇Jκ=φ₅₂ and ∇Jξ=φ₆₂, it follows that
φ₉₁+ξ=∇J(3Jξ/4−3Θ/2−11Iκ/24).
In particular H+2Iξ=∇J(3IJξ/2−3IΘ−3I²κ/4). This supplies a rational logarithmic relative correction and leaves a multiple of ξ. No solution of the full mixed source follows just from this identity.
