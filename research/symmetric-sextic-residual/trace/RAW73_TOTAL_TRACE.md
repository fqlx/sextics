# Raw73 total-trace reduction and its open obligations

This note does not compute a source trace, prove a source match, or construct the protected primitive. The raw circuit is `raw73-outer-source-circuit.json`; the 60 fixed-μ coefficient forms are `raw-fixed-mu-det6-forms.json`.

Let K=Q(λ,r,s), let E₁,…,E₇₃ be the literal raw equations, J their 73-variable Jacobian determinant, and U the product of J and every polynomial denominator/open needed by the source circuit. Define the explicitly localized algebra

    A=K[x₁,…,x₇₃,t]/(E₁,…,E₇₃,tU−1).

Its augmented Jacobian is UJ, which is a unit in A. The Jacobian criterion and the standard theorem that an étale algebra over a field is a finite product of finite separable field extensions imply A is finite étale, possibly the zero algebra. See https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00U3. The independent replay `raw73_units_seed.py` verifies that all 73 equations vanish at the recorded F37 seed, J=10, and the four recorded source-unit nodes are [25,13,23,32]. If every additional factor of U is likewise nonzero at that seed, the étale open-image argument supplies nonemptiness in characteristic zero. These statements concern only the selected open: they do not include roots excluded by U. The product decomposition is classical and is not yet source-bound in Lean.

For a polynomial numerator N and a unit denominator V in A, the ordinary complete trace satisfies

    Tr_A/K(N/(JV)) = Res_(E,tU−1)(U N/V),

where the right side sums local Grothendieck residues at all points of A, including every field component, with the coordinate and equation order fixed. This equation is an algebraic rewriting; it does not evaluate the residue. For rational circuit factors, V must be explicitly invertible on the chosen open. A single selected field factor instead requires an idempotent or independent factor construction.

The literal first 70 contact rows have 2 linear and 68 quadratic equations in their 71 raw-slice coordinates; the last three point rows have degrees 3,3,4. Affine Bézout therefore bounds the number of isolated geometric raw73 roots, hence the rank of A, by 36·2^68. At the 70-row minor-open, any contact curve component is one-dimensional and its projective degree is at most 2^68. Each affine coefficient has pole order at most this degree on its normalized compactification; the degree-5 raw det6 coefficient times a coefficient differential consequently has pole order at most 6·2^68+1. This does not bound the full source integrand until the point-chart, source denominators, non-étale boundary, and their intersections are controlled. `raw73_infinity_linear.py` verifies term by term over Z[λ,r,s], after confirming true polynomial degrees, that the naive projective homogenization of the 73 raw rows has an excess P40 at infinity. This does not establish that the P40 survives saturation. No vanishing or boundary-free toric formula follows from naive degree counting.

The leading 70×71 q-only norm Jacobian factors through 14 columns on P40. `raw73_infinity_linear.py` checks termwise over Z[λ,r,s] that the remaining A/B and free columns vanish there, and that the τ column is the velocity-weighted combination of the first 14 quadric-coefficient columns. Multilinearity therefore puts every 70×70 contact cofactor in I^56. The homogeneous leading coefficients of f1,f2,A,B also vanish there, so f1,f2,h,k give four rows in the boundary ideal. In a coefficient of the det6 one-form at most one row is used for the coefficient-direction derivative; at least three of those four rows remain among the spatial derivatives. Thus each degree-5 form coefficient lies in I³, and its contraction with contact cofactors lies in I^59. These numerator-side valuations alone do not prove boundary residues vanish.

For the unreduced source factors, the same script independently checks that every inverse operand depends only on λ and every unknown they use is in I. Their individual degree bounds are (18,16,14,12,16,14,12); a degree-d source homogenization is consequently in I^d. The homogenized 73 equations have exact I-orders 70×1 and (3,3,4), total 80. The raw affine meromorphic 73-form has numerator of degree at most 73+d and order at least 59+d. Since Σdeg(E)=148, affine volume contributes z^−74, and the P40 blowup has discrepancy 32, its pullback has generic exceptional order at least (59+d)+(148−(73+d)−74)+32−80=12. Hence it has no pole along the first exceptional divisor, assuming the raw circuit's stated meromorphic residue representation. This does not control poles at strict-transform intersections, further exceptional divisors, or other boundary components, and does not evaluate a source trace.

At P40 the first-order norm variation has the form g1·δf1+g2·δf2 plus a z term, while A²−B² begins in order2. Therefore its normal Jacobian has rank at most 14+1=15. `raw73_boundary_rank.py` gives an explicit F101 point and a 15×15 minor equal to 70, so this upper bound is attained generically in characteristic zero. The 70×31 normal matrix has substantial kernel; the first blowup does not by itself resolve the norm incidence.

For an actual algebraic certificate, a finite monomial set B connected to 1 could be supplied with terminating ideal-verified border rewrites xᵢbⱼ=Σ cᵢⱼₖbₖ. Commuting matrices Mᵢ satisfying the equations and bⱼ(M)e₁=eⱼ would prove independence; the rewrites prove spanning. Including U⁻¹ in this certificate yields the full open algebra, and matrix traces then compute its moments. No such basis or source traces are currently known.

The fixed μ is an integer lift of a mod-37 ambient functional, not a characteristic-zero Jacobian-residue functional. The trace is linear in μ. Thus a generic global residue theorem or finite-dimensionality alone cannot imply its nonzero protected target: the zero functional obeys the same incidence and boundary hypotheses but gives zero moments. An exact calculation using the actual μ, or a separately certified inverse/correction, is necessary.
