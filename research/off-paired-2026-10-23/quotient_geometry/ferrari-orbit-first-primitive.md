# An exact first primitive on a coefficient orbit

This identity applies along a coefficient orbit, not to the entire physical
four-form. Its unintegrated correction is a rational logarithmic differential.

Use the Möbius invariants
\[
 I=S^2+12R,\quad J=72SR-27T^2-2S^3,\quad
 \lambda=q^2+2S/3,\quad D=3\lambda^2-I/3.
\]
The Ferrari equation gives
\[
 \lambda^3-I\lambda/3+J/27=0,
 \qquad M(I)=M(\lambda)=0.
\]
Put \(W=T/q\). On a fixed \((I,\lambda)\) orbit,
\[
 W^2=Q(S)=\frac{4S^2+6\lambda S+9\lambda^2-3I}{9}.
\]
The actual pair source coefficient has \(q_{SS}=-2qN/D^3\), where
\[
 N=I^2/18-5IS^2/54+IS\lambda/9+I\lambda^2/6
 -4S^3\lambda/9-7S^2\lambda^2/6-S\lambda^3.
\]
Define
\[
 P=-\lambda S^2/3-(5I+33\lambda^2)S/48
   +\lambda(-I+51\lambda^2)/64,
\]
\[
 k=(4I^2+9I\lambda^2+81\lambda^4)/192,
 \qquad \Psi=2W+4S/3+\lambda.
\]
Direct polynomial identities give
\[
 N=P'Q+PQ'/2+k,\qquad
 d\log\Psi=\frac{2\,dS}{3W}.
\]
Consequently the exact coefficient-orbit identity is
\[
 \boxed{q_{SS}\frac{dS}{3T}
 =-\frac{2}{3D^3}\,d(PW)-\frac{k}{D^3}\,d\log\Psi.}
\]
Only rational functions and rational logarithmic differentials occur. No
choice of a logarithm is part of the identity.

The physical density is essential. For instance, using coordinates
\((S,I,J,z)\), its inverse-Jacobian includes \(1/(u^8Z)\), where
\(u^6=(I-S^2)/12\) and \(Z=b^6-a^6\). Multiplying the boxed formula by
this density produces its derivative as a further correction. Thus the
boxed rational first primitive and logarithmic correction do not yet
construct a primitive for the source. A successful construction must
cancel that physical correction using an additional direction.
