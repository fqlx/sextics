# Stopped relative bare-C seed

The standalone bare-C ODE and its symmetric a=1,z=0 seed were stopped after
the parent scope check: with independent horizontal currents set to zero,
they reconstruct a primitive on the already excluded fixed physical
X1/X0,Y1/X0 surface. Jacobian transport currents generated mechanically by
that reduction do not create an independent transverse current.

The files `symmetric_bare_C_seed.py` and its results are retained only as
internal triage. They are not a new obstruction or a live positive target.
The seed script finished its one specialization while the stop message was
being delivered; no further seed expansion or generic ODE solve was pursued.
The complete scalar PDE with genuinely independent horizontal U,W remains
the relevant compact construction problem.
