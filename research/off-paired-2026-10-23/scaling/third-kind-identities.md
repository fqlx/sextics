# Sparse identities for the added third-kind differential

These identities enlarge the constructive tools but do not give the physical
primitive. Work in the weighted gauge \(H=1,C=t\), on
\(Z^2=(1+tp)^2-4p^6\), with \(\omega_j=p^jdp/Z\).
The compact basis remains \(j=0,1,3,4\); add \(\omega_2\), whose residues
at the two points at infinity are constant. Put \(v=\nabla_t\omega_2\).

The reconstructed columns give, in cohomology,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_t\omega_0&=\frac{4t^2}{9}v-\frac13\omega_1,\\
\nabla_t\omega_1&=-\frac{2t}{3}v,\\
\nabla_t\omega_3&=-\frac{3}{2t}v+\frac1{2t}\omega_3,\\
\nabla_t\omega_4&=\frac9{4t^2}v-\frac3{4t^2}\omega_3+\frac1t\omega_4.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus useful rational gauges include

\[
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_t\left(\omega_1+\frac{2t}{3}\omega_2\right)
 &=\frac23\omega_2,\\
\nabla_t\left(\omega_0-\frac{4t^2}{9}\omega_2\right)
 &=-\frac13\omega_1-\frac{8t}{9}\omega_2,\\
\nabla_t\left(\frac{\omega_4}{t}
              +\frac{3\omega_3}{2t^2}\right)
 &=-\frac{3\omega_3}{t^3}.
\end{aligned}
\]

All scalar derivatives in these formulas matter. In a physical current, the
coefficient of the nonzero residue of \(\omega_2\) must have zero ordinary
divergence; otherwise the current adds an unwanted third-kind source.

## Explicit logarithmic identities on the universal curve

Let \(r=(H+Cp+Z)/2\), so \(r+p^6/r=H+Cp\). Relative differentiation gives

\[
d_p\log(r/p^3)=-\left(\frac{3H}{p}+2C\right)\frac{dp}{Z}.
\]

Its divisor is three times the difference of the two points above \(p=0\).
These points therefore give a distinguished three-torsion class. They are
different from the two infinity sections defining \(\omega_2\).

On the full universal curve, retaining \(dH,dC\) components, one has the
exact rational-form identities

\[
(dH+p\,dC)\wedge p^j\frac{dp}{Z}
=\frac{dr}{r}\wedge p^jdp
=-d\left(\frac{p^{j+1}}{j+1}\frac{dr}{r}\right),\qquad j\ge0.
\]

The logarithm notation denotes the rational differential \(dr/r\); no
logarithmic function is admitted as a primitive. These identities provide
rational primitives for the displayed mixed two-forms. They do not yet
integrate the physical four-form or justify omitting its normal correction.
