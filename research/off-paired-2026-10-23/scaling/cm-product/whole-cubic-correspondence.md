# The whole cubic over a fixed CM elliptic product

This gives a finite correspondence carrying the entire physical cubic source.
It does not provide a scalar primitive or a contraction.

Use the notation of `../../quotient_geometry/cubic-fermat-product-source.md`.
The fixed Fermat factors have coordinates `(xi,eta)` and `(nu,varpi)`, and
`alpha^6=H-1`, `beta^6=C-1`. Put

\[
 q=\xi\eta,\quad U_1=q^{-2},\quad
 V_1=(\xi^6-\eta^6)/q^3.
\]

Then `V1²=U1³-4` and

\[
 \frac{d\xi}{\eta^5}=\frac{dU_1}{2V_1}.
\]

The same formula with `(nu,varpi)` and `r=nu varpi` gives the second copy
of the fixed `j=0` elliptic curve. The first map has degree twelve: its
deck transformations are generated by
`(xi,eta)->(zeta xi,zeta^-1 eta)` and `(xi,eta)->(eta,-xi)`.
Both fix `U1,V1`. The group relations are `a^6=1`, `j²=a³`,
`j a j^-1=a^-1`.

The original Fermat product is recovered over the elliptic product by

\[
 q^2=1/U_1,\quad 2\xi^6=1+V_1q^3,\quad \eta=q/\xi,
\]
\[
 r^2=1/U_2,\quad 2\nu^6=1+V_2r^3,\quad \varpi=r/\nu.
\]

Writing
`b1=alpha beta/2`, `b2=alpha^4 beta²/2`, `b3=alpha² beta/3`, the complete
coupling is

\[
 B=b_1\xi\nu+b_2qr\xi^2+b_3qr/\nu.
\]

Retain the cubic `H+Bp+Cp³=0` and `g=3Cp²+B`. This is an explicit finite
cover of degree at most `12²*3=432` over
`E0 × E0 × Spec(k(H,C,alpha,beta))`. There is no replacement of `B` by an
independent coefficient. The exact source on this cover is

\[
 -\frac{\alpha^{-4}\beta^{-4}}{144}\frac{Bp}{g^3}
 dH\wedge dC\wedge\frac{dU_1}{V_1}\wedge\frac{dU_2}{V_2}.
\]

The scalar still lives in the finite cover. Its trace over the cubic
extension is zero. Consequently the trace to the CM product does not
recover the original source, and the elliptic differential identities
alone cannot be inserted into the accepted scalar contraction.

## Exact angle derivatives for the correction

Let the elliptic dual derivations be

\[
 \delta_1=\eta^5\partial_\xi-\xi^5\partial_\eta,
 \qquad
 \delta_2=\varpi^5\partial_\nu-\nu^5\partial_\varpi.
\]

They give `delta_i(U_i)=2V_i`, `delta_i(V_i)=3U_i²`, and preserve `H,C`.
Their complete coupling derivatives are

\[
 \delta_1B=b_1\nu\eta^5+
 b_2\nu\varpi\xi^2(3\eta^6-\xi^6)
 +b_3\varpi(\eta^6-\xi^6),
\]
\[
 \delta_2B=b_1\xi\varpi^5+
 b_2\xi^3\eta(\varpi^6-\nu^6)-b_3\xi\eta\nu^5.
\]

Their extensions to the physical cubic satisfy
`delta_i(p)=-p delta_i(B)/g`. Thus, for any rational coefficient potential
`Q(H,B,C,p)`, the exact angle derivative is
`delta_i(Q)=delta_i(B) D_B(Q)`; this retains the full coupling derivative.
For example, `Q_B=-p/(2g)` has `D_B(Q_B)=Bp/g³`, so its angle derivative
is `delta_i(B)` times the actual source scalar. Dividing by `delta_i(B)`
introduces the explicit correction
`-Q_B delta_i²(B)/(delta_i(B))²`; it is not a primitive.

The companion checker verifies nine exact identities. This representation
is useful if a future algebraic companion can cancel those cross
derivatives on the actual finite cover.
