\[

\[

\]

The excluded equations are specifically

\[
\operatorname{div}_{dx\,dy\,dz} V=0,\qquad
2C_c V(H_c)-3H_c V(C_c)=2A.
\]

Their product-rule consequence

\[
\operatorname{div}\left(\frac{C_c^6}{H_c^4}V\right)
=-\frac{4AC_c^5}{H_c^5}

has no regularity or specialization assumption on \(V\) at \(H_c=0\).
It would make the actual rational three-form
\(AC_c^5\,dx\wedge dy\wedge dz/H_c^5\) exact.

the gradient has no projective zero. At \(w=1\), simultaneous zero gradient
and nonzero \(xyz\) would imply \(xyz=-4\) and
\(x^6=y^6=z^6=1/3\), contradicting \(4096=1/27\). A zero among
\(x,y,z\) also contradicts the \(w\) derivative. The diagonal group fixes
the surface and projective volume, and its invariant projection of the
actual numerator is \(5w^{11}x^7y^7z/162\).
