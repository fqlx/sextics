## Normalization retains the actual source

Use one cube root \(\rho^3=H/C\), and set

\[
p=\rho r,\qquad j=\frac B{C\rho^2}.
\]

Then the actual cubic and source become

\[
f=C\rho^3(1+jr+r^3),\qquad
g=C\rho^2(j+3r^2),\qquad
HC\,R=\frac{jr}{(j+3r^2)^3}.
\]

This single-root prescription avoids any unproved compatibility between
independent cube roots of \(H\) and \(C\). The normalized source carries
the original physical factor: \(R\Omega=(HC\,R)\Omega/(HC)\).

On the fixed elliptic curve \(E:Y^2=X^3-16\), put

\[
r=-4/X,\qquad
j=\frac{X^3-64}{4X^2}.
\]

The identity \(r^3+jr+1=0\) holds exactly. The actual source and the
canonical potential are

\[
W:=HC\,R=-\frac{64X^3(X^3-64)}{(X^3+128)^3},\qquad
F:=-\frac{H}{pg}=\frac{X^3}{X^3+128}.
\]

## The complete degree-three kernel and S3 deck action

The map

\[
U=\frac{X^3-64}{X^2}=4j,\qquad
V=-\frac{Y(X^3+128)}{X^3}
\]

satisfies \(V^2=U^3+432\). It extends to a morphism of smooth elliptic
curves \(E\to E'\) taking the identity to the identity, hence is an
isogeny. Its full kernel is

\[
\{O,P,-P\},\qquad P=(0,4i).
\]

Indeed the horizontal tangent at \(P\) has intersection multiplicity three,
so \(3P=O\). The only poles of \(U\) are \(O,P,-P\), each of order two.
Since the target \(U\)-coordinate has degree two, the map has degree three.
Its fiber over the target identity contains exactly those three points; in
characteristic zero the kernel is reduced. This accounts for the whole
kernel, rather than an assumed subgroup of a larger isogeny kernel.

Fix the pullback convention \(T h(Q)=h(Q+P)\). With \(a=4i\), the chord
formula gives

\[
T X=-\frac{32+2aY}{X^2},\qquad
T Y=-a-\frac{(Y-a)TX}{X}.
\]

The independent quotient-polynomial audit verifies that these formulas
preserve \(E\), that \(T^3=1\), and that both \(U,V\) are fixed. It also
checks the inverse translation and all three roots
\(r,Tr,T^2r\): their sum is zero, pairwise product sum is \(j\), and product
is \(-1\).

Let \(\sigma(X,Y)=(X,-Y)\). It fixes \(r,j\), negates \(V\), and satisfies
\(\sigma T\sigma=T^{-1}\). The rational map \(X\mapsto j\) has degree
three, and \(E\to\mathbb P^1_X\) has degree two. Thus the map
\(E\to\mathbb P^1_j\) has degree six; the six distinct automorphisms
generated by \(T,\sigma\) give its full S3 Galois group. The original cubic
field in \(r\) is the \(\sigma\)-fixed subfield.

The cube-root ambiguity is also explicit. For \(w^3=1\),

\[
A:(\rho,X,Y)\longmapsto(w\rho,wX,Y).
\]

It changes \(j\) to \(wj\), fixes the physical \(H,B,p\), fixes \(W,F\),
and commutes with \(T\). The checker verifies these statements using exact
monomial characters modulo three. This automorphism changes the normalized
parameter; it is distinct from the three-point kernel over fixed \(U,V\).

## Whole-kernel traces and difference-operator inverses

The explicit rational identities over all three kernel translates are

\[
F+TF+T^2F=1,\qquad W+TW+T^2W=0.
\]

Because \(H,C\) are fixed by the deck action, the second identity is also
the exact physical coefficient trace \(R+TR+T^2R=0\). It is not a trace
over just one selected sheet or a trace of an unrelated universal source.

In the group ring, write \(N=1+T+T^2\). The exact operator identities are

\[
(1-T)\frac{2+T}{3}=1-\frac N3,
\qquad
(1+T)(-T)=1-N.
\]

Hence \((2+T)/3\) is the inverse of \(1-T\) on \(\ker N\), and \(-T\)
is the inverse of \(1+T\) there. They apply to \(W\), \(R\), and
\(F-1/3\). Both sides commute, so these are left and right inverses on
the trace-free part.

These identities alone do not construct a rational de Rham primitive of
\(R\Omega\). An actual source-carrying current, or an explicit compatible
differential homotopy with its pole and descent conditions checked, remains
necessary. In particular, trivial action of translations on the cohomology
of the complete elliptic curve does not automatically supply a rational
homotopy for forms with the source's poles and varying physical factors.
