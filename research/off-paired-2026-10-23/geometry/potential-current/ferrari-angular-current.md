# A source-carrying angular current criterion on the Ferrari pair cover

No rational fields satisfying this criterion have been found here. This is a
restricted constructive interface, not a certificate or nonexistence claim.

Let the Ferrari pair coordinate satisfy

\[
 q^2(q^2+S)^2=u^6(4q^2+e^2),
\]

where

\[
 e=zab/2,\quad S=1+z^6+a^6+b^6+uz/2+uab/3.
\]

Its weighted scaling has \((e,u,S,q)=(3,2,6,3)\); thus \(q_S\) has weight
\(-3\). A physical current \(Tq_S\), relative to the volume
\(du\,dz\,da\,db\), differentiates to \(Bq_{SS}\) if

\[
 T(e)=3he,\quad T(u)=2hu,\quad T(S)=6hS+B,\quad\operatorname{div}T=3h.
\]

The desired affine pair source uses \(B=-1/3\).

Take

\[
 E=2u\partial_u+z\partial_z+a\partial_a+b\partial_b,\quad
 L_1=z\partial_z-a\partial_a,\quad
 L_2=z\partial_z-b\partial_b.
\]

The angular fields preserve \(u,e\), commute, and have zero divergence.
Write \(T=hE+v_1L_1+v_2L_2\). Then the exact remaining equations are

\[
 v_1 A_1+v_2 A_2=B-hK,\qquad
 L_1(v_1)+L_2(v_2)=-2h-E(h),
\]

\[
 A_1=6(z^6-a^6)+uz/2-uab/3,\quad
 A_2=6(z^6-b^6)+uz/2-uab/3,
\]

\[
 K=E(S)-6S=-6-3uz/2-2uab/3.
\]

If \(E(h)=-2h\), the divergence equation is satisfied by
\(v_1=L_2(F),v_2=-L_1(F)\). The source equation becomes the explicit scalar
Hamiltonian equation

\[
 L_2(F)L_1(S)-L_1(F)L_2(S)=B-hK.
\]

No primitive follows merely by choosing \(h=B/K\): that choice generally
fails its required Euler weight and introduces the uncancelled divergence.
