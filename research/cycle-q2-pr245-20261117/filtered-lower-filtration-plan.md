# Exact lower-filtration obligation

Prepared for fqlx with OpenAI ChatGPT assistance. This is a proof plan, not a checked theorem or protected solution.

The checked candidate is c=(-a,eta,0,0) in total degree three of the four-chart algebraic de Rham Cech complex, with total differential delta+(-1)^p d on Cech degree p.

If c were the full boundary of (v,u,f) in C^0 Omega^2 + C^1 Omega^1 + C^2 O, its components would be:

    d v = -a
    delta v - d u = eta
    delta u + d f = 0
    delta f = 0.

It suffices to establish on the actual original cover:

1. Every O-valued two-cocycle f is delta g for an actual one-cochain g.
2. For every Omega^1-valued one-cocycle u', there is a vertex one-form h with d u' = delta(d h).

Indeed subtract Dg from (v,u,f); the new lower terms are (u+d g,0). This one-cochain u' is delta-closed. The second assertion gives delta(v-d h)=eta, while d(v-d h)=-a. Thus the already nonzero filtered test would be the filtered boundary of v-d h, a contradiction.

A concrete route to (1) is Laurent Cech vanishing for O on the monic weighted sextic. Drafts GeometricHodgeTwistTorusChart and its dependencies contain such a calculation, but its binding and compilation for this original literal cover still have to be checked.

A concrete route to (2) is to prove H^1(Omega^1_T) is generated by the closed logarithmic hyperplane cocycle. The dual Euler sequence with O(-1)^4 plus O(-2), followed by the hypersurface conormal sequence with O(-6), reduces this to intermediate vanishing of the integral twists and a precise comparison of the connecting class with dlog(X_j/X_i). No existing checked lemma supplies this identification yet. It cannot be replaced by assuming degeneration or defining the full H^3 to be the filtered quotient.

After this fixed-fiber nonvanishing, full invariant Gysin spanning over the coefficient family, specialization, and the protected generic residue comparison remain separate requirements.
